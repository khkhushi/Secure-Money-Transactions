# Secure-Money-Transactions
The main objective of this project is to develop a system, which is used for ATM security applications. In these systems, Bankers will collect the customer data and mobile number while opening the accounts then customer only access ATM machine. The growth in electronic transactions has resulted in a greater demand for fast and accurate user identification and authentication. Access codes for buildings, bank accounts and computer systems often use personal identification numbers (PIN's) for identification and security clearances.
Today, ATMs and Credit cards are used for the purpose of money transactions which play a vital role in the nature of trade. The weaknesses of existing authentication scheme such as password and PIN number caused the leakage of information stored in ATM smartcard which lead to the loss of money in bank account and private information misuses. To overcome this shortcoming of piracy in money transactions, we propose some methods so that we can overcome these problems and the drawback found in existing technology of ATM machines.
In particular, we develop a prototype model for the utilization of GUI equipped ATM to provide security solutions against most of the well-known breaches, and to ensure that such security approach will be accepted by the majority of users.
